## Data and artwork

- **Game catalogue:** the bundled NES game list is derived from the
  [Libretro database](https://github.com/libretro/libretro-database).
- **Box art, title screens and screenshots** are downloaded on request from
  the [Libretro thumbnails](https://github.com/libretro-thumbnails) project and
  belong to their respective owners. None are bundled with the app.
- **Game details:** [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org) (CC0) and
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org) summaries (CC BY-SA 4.0), credited
  with a link on each game that uses them.
- **Cheat catalogue:** fetched on request from the Libretro database.
