OpenNES is a free, open-source emulator made for one system: the NES and Famicom. It has no ads, no account and no tracking.

OpenNES does not include any games. You play game files that you own and add yourself.

Built for phones
- A touch controller styled after the original pad, with separate layouts for portrait and landscape
- Move and resize every button, tune the D-pad, and pick your own colors
- Works with Bluetooth and USB gamepads

Your library
- Point OpenNES at a folder and it finds your games
- Box art, descriptions, favorites, search and play time
- Fix a wrong match yourself, or set your own cover

Play your way
- Battery saves, save states and optional autosave
- Rewind the last few seconds
- Fast-forward, display filters and Game Genie codes
- Touchscreen Zapper for light-gun games

Optional online features
- RetroAchievements: sign in to see and earn achievements while you play
- RomM: browse and download games from your own RomM server

Everything works offline. Online features are off until you turn them on, and the privacy policy lists exactly what each one connects to.

OpenNES is free software under the GNU GPL version 3, based on Lemuroid and the FCEUmm core. Source code: https://github.com/JPLuker/OpenNES
